# Contributing

Suggestions are welcome, through issues rather than pull requests for anything beyond typo fixes. This is a single-maintainer curriculum with a deliberate point of view, so I curate rather than merge.

## Suggesting a resource

Open an issue with the link, the stage it belongs in, and two or three sentences on what it teaches that the existing list does not. The quality bar:

- Durable sources preferred: official documentation, peer-reviewed papers, or engineering blogs with a track record. No marketing pages, no course affiliate links.
- The link must be reachable. CI checks every link in the repo, and a resource that dies gets removed.
- It must earn its place. Each stage lists four to eight resources on purpose. If yours goes in, something else may come out, so say what you would drop.

## Reporting problems

Broken links, factual errors, and exercises that no longer work against the referenced repos are all worth an issue. For factual errors, cite a source.

## What I will not take

Framework advocacy without a workload argument, resource lists without curation, and rewrites of the opinions. The opinions are the product. If yours differ, fork it. The licence is CC BY 4.0 and forking with credit is an intended use.
